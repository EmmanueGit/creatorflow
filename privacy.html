Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how CreatorFlow handles information when you use the service or connect third-party social media accounts.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, CreatorFlow may process:

  • Account information you provide to CreatorFlow, such as an email address and workspace information.
  • Basic profile information returned by connected social media platforms after you authorize access.
  • OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens needed to keep authorized integrations connected.
  • Videos, captions, titles, descriptions, scheduling information, and other content you choose to upload or publish.
  • Technical information necessary to operate and secure the service, such as logs and error information.

3. TikTok Data

If you connect TikTok, CreatorFlow may request the user.info.basic permission to identify the connected TikTok account and display basic account information inside CreatorFlow.

If you use TikTok content upload features, CreatorFlow may request the video.upload permission so that content you explicitly select can be uploaded to TikTok for review and completion within TikTok.

CreatorFlow only accesses TikTok information and permissions that you authorize through TikTok's official authorization process.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Authenticate users and operate CreatorFlow.
  • Connect authorized social media accounts.
  • Display connected account information.
  • Upload, schedule, or manage content at your request.
  • Maintain security, troubleshoot errors, and improve reliability.

5. Sharing of Information

CreatorFlow does not sell personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party platforms only when needed to perform actions you request, such as connecting an account or uploading content. Those platforms process information under their own privacy policies.

6. Data Storage and Security

CreatorFlow may store account details, authorization tokens, content metadata, and related information required to provide the service. Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect this information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain authorized integrations, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes. Stored data may be removed when it is no longer needed.

8. Disconnecting Social Accounts

You may disconnect a connected social media account through CreatorFlow where that option is available, or revoke CreatorFlow's authorization through the relevant social platform's account settings.

Revoking access may prevent CreatorFlow from using that integration until you authorize it again.

9. Data Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your use of CreatorFlow by contacting emmanuelchristophermpagulayan@gmail.com. Some information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and record-keeping purposes.

10. Children's Privacy

CreatorFlow is not intended for children who are not legally permitted to use the connected social media services. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

11. Third-Party Services

CreatorFlow may link to or integrate with external services. CreatorFlow is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party platforms. You should review the privacy policies of each service you connect.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date shown at the top will be updated when material changes are made.

13. Contact
